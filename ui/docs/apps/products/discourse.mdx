Discourse is a discussion forum. Conversations are organized into categories and tags, members follow the topics that interest them, and chat channels sit next to the forum.

## Settings

| Setting   | Meaning                                              |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Hostname  | The app's address                                    |
| Your name | How you appear to others; your username is made from it |
| Password  | The password of your Discourse administrator account |

The password is used once, when the app first starts. To change it, or your name, later, use Discourse's own account preferences. Changing them with **Settings** afterwards has no effect.

## First sign-in

1. Select **Open** on the app's row.
2. Select **Log In** and sign in with the email address of your Freepod account and the password from the settings.

Your account is the forum's administrator. Discourse makes your username from your name; change it, or your name, under **Preferences → Account**. Discourse's **Launch in 3 easy steps** panel on the home page walks through choosing a theme, inviting people and starting the first topics. Set the forum's name and who may join under **Admin → Settings**.

## Changing the address

Discourse writes the forum's address into every post when it is created. If you change **Hostname** later, links and images in existing posts keep pointing at the old address and stop working. Choose the address before your forum fills up.

The app's address and email delivery are managed by Freepod, so Discourse's admin settings don't show them.

## Backups

Discourse makes backups of the whole forum, its database and its uploads, under **Admin → Backups**. Each backup counts toward the app's storage. A backup can be restored into any other Discourse forum.

Discourse makes no automatic backups: create one with **Backup** when you need it. To have Discourse make one on a schedule, set **backup frequency** in the backup settings; it keeps the five most recent.

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows Discourse's uploads and backups, read-only: `uploads/` holds uploaded files and images, and `backups/` the backups made in **Admin → Backups**.

## Email

Discourse sends email, such as invitations, notifications and summaries, from `discourse@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).

## Mobile apps

Discourse Hub for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/discourse-hub/id1173672076) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.discourse) connects to your forum by its address.
