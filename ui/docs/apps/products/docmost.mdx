Docmost is a wiki where several people can edit the same page at once. Pages are organized in spaces, and each space has its own members and permissions.

## Settings

| Setting   | Meaning                              |
|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| Hostname  | The app's address                    |
| Your name | How you appear to others in Docmost  |
| Password  | The password of your Docmost account |

The password is used once, when the app first starts. To change it, or your name, later, use Docmost's account settings. Changing them with **Settings** afterwards has no effect.

## First sign-in

1. Select **Open** on the app's row.
2. Sign in with the email address of your Freepod account and the password from the settings.

Your account owns the Docmost workspace, which starts out named "My workspace". Rename it in Docmost's **Settings → General**, and invite other people with **Invite People** in the sidebar.

Some features listed in Docmost's settings, such as single sign-on, two-factor authentication and API keys, need a Docmost Enterprise license, which Docmost sells separately. Without one they stay unavailable.

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows Docmost's uploaded files and images, read-only. They are stored in folders named by Docmost's internal IDs.

## Email

Docmost sends email, such as invitations and password resets, from `docmost@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).
