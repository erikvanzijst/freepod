OpenProject is project management for teams: work packages, Gantt charts, agile boards, time and cost tracking, meetings, and documents you write together in real time.

## Settings

| Setting   | Meaning                                                |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Hostname  | The app's address                                      |
| Your name | How you appear to others in OpenProject                |
| Password  | The password of your OpenProject administrator account |

The password is used once, when the app first starts. To change it, or your name, later, use OpenProject's account settings. Changing them with **Settings** afterwards has no effect.

## First sign-in

1. Select **Open** on the app's row.
2. Sign in with the username `admin` and the password from the settings.

Your account is OpenProject's administrator, with the email address of your Freepod account. Invite other people from **Administration → Users**. You can rename your own username there too.

Some features, such as the team planner, placeholder users and single sign-on, need an OpenProject Enterprise plan, which OpenProject sells separately. Without one they stay unavailable.

The app's address and email delivery are managed by Freepod, so OpenProject's administration shows them as read-only.

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows OpenProject's uploaded attachments, read-only. They are stored in folders named by OpenProject's internal IDs.

## Email

OpenProject sends email, such as invitations and notifications, from `openproject@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).
