Immich is a photo and video library with mobile apps that back up a phone's camera roll.

## Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Hostname | The app's address |

## First sign-in

The first account created in Immich becomes its administrator. Open the app soon after launching it:

1. Select **Open** on the app's card.
2. Select **Getting Started**.
3. Enter an email address, a password and your name, and select **Sign up**.
4. Sign in with that email address and password.

The administrator creates accounts for other people in Immich's administration settings.

## Mobile apps

Install Immich from the App Store or Google Play and enter `https://<hostname>` as the server URL. See [Immich: Mobile app](https://immich.app/docs/features/mobile-app).

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows the photo library, read-only.

## Email

Immich sends email, such as password resets, from `immich@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).
