Immich is a photo and video library with mobile apps that back up a phone's camera roll.

## Settings

| Setting  | Meaning           |
|----------|-------------------|
| Hostname | The app's address |

## Signing in

Immich uses Freepod accounts: everyone signs in with **Sign in with Freepod**, and nobody has a separate Immich password. You sign in with your own Freepod account and are Immich's administrator.

## Adding people

You decide who can use your Immich from the Freepod dashboard:

1. Select **Access** on the app's row.
2. Enter the person's email address under **Add someone by email** and select **Add**.

Freepod emails them a link to your Immich. They sign in with a Freepod account that uses that address, creating one if they don't have one yet, and Immich sets up their account the first time they sign in.

To take someone's access away, remove them from the list in **Access**. Freepod signs them out of Immich; their account and photos stay, and they can sign in again if you add them back.

## Mobile apps

Install Immich from the App Store or Google Play, enter `https://<hostname>` as the server URL and select **Sign in with Freepod**. See [Immich: Mobile app](https://immich.app/docs/features/mobile-app).

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows the photo library, read-only.

## Email

Immich sends email, such as notices about shared albums, from `immich@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).
