BookStack is a wiki that organizes pages into shelves, books and chapters.

## Settings

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Hostname | The app's address |
| Your name | How you appear to others in BookStack |
| Password | The password of your BookStack account |

The password is used once, when the app first starts. To change it, or your name, later, use your BookStack profile. Changing them with **Edit** afterwards has no effect.

## First sign-in

1. Select **Open** on the app's card.
2. Sign in with the email address of your Freepod account and the password from the settings.

Your account is BookStack's administrator. Invite other people from BookStack's **Settings → Users**.

## File access

[SFTP](../file-access.md) shows BookStack's uploaded files and images, read-only.

## Email

BookStack sends email, such as invitations and password resets, from `bookstack@freepod.eu`. See [Email](../email.md).
