# Get started

Freepod runs apps from its catalog, such as photo libraries, file storage and password managers, on hostnames of your own. You choose an app and a plan; Freepod installs, runs and updates it.

## 1. Create an account

1. Go to [freepod.eu](https://freepod.eu) and select **Create account**.
2. Register with an email address and password, or with a Google account.
3. If you registered with an email address, open the verification link Freepod sends you.

## 2. Choose your subdomain

After you sign in, the dashboard asks you to choose a subdomain, for example `alice.freepod.eu`. Your apps get addresses under it, such as `photos.alice.freepod.eu`.

1. Enter a name: 2 to 63 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, starting and ending with a letter or digit.
2. Select **Claim this name** and confirm.

The subdomain cannot be changed or released later. **Not now** postpones the choice until you launch your first app. It is also available under **Settings → Subdomain**.

## 3. Launch an app

1. Under **Available apps** on the dashboard, select an app.
2. Select a plan.
3. Fill in the settings. **Hostname** is the app's address: enter a name, such as `photos`, to use `photos.alice.freepod.eu`, or select **Custom domain** to use a domain you own. See [Hostname](managing/hostname.md) and [Custom domain](managing/custom-domain.mdx).
4. The first time, accept the Terms of Service.
5. Select **Launch**. For a paid plan, you are sent to a payment page first.

The app appears under **Your apps**. Its status changes from `provisioning` to `ready` when it is running; this usually takes a few minutes.

## 4. Open the app

Select **Open** on the app's card. Most apps need a first-time setup, such as creating an admin account. The [product guides](/apps/products) describe it for each app.
