# Plans and billing

Freepod is in beta. Prices and billing are subject to change; the [pricing page](app:/#pricing) shows current prices.

## Catalog apps

Each app runs on a plan, chosen when it is launched. The plan sets the app's monthly price and how much storage it includes. Paid plans are charged monthly; at launch you are sent to a payment page to set up payment.

- **Waiting for payment** on an app's card means payment has not been completed yet. The app starts once it has.
- **Payment issue** means a recent payment failed.

To change an app's plan, or for any question about payments, contact [support@freepod.eu](mailto:support@freepod.eu).

## Developer deployments

Deployments made with the `freepod` command-line client are billed by usage. See [Usage and billing](/developers/usage-and-billing) in the developer documentation.

## Usage

**Settings → Usage** shows the CPU and memory your apps and deployments used, and its cost, per app or per resource. Usage is measured hourly; amounts exclude VAT.
