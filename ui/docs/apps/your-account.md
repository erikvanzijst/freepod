# Your account

How you sign in to Freepod is managed on your account page, [keycloak.freepod.eu/realms/freepod/account](https://keycloak.freepod.eu/realms/freepod/account). Its **Account security** section lists your password, passkeys, authenticator app and linked Google account.

## Sign in with a passkey

A passkey signs you in with your device's fingerprint, face or screen lock instead of your password. It is kept by your device, your browser or your password manager, and works only on Freepod's sign-in page.

To add one:

1. On your account page, go to **Account security → Signing in**.
2. Under **Passkey**, select **Set up Passkey**, then **Register**, and confirm on your device.
3. Name the passkey, for example `Laptop`. Each passkey on your account needs a different name.

To sign in with it, select the **Email** field on the sign-in page and choose the passkey, or select **Sign in with Passkey** to use a passkey on your phone or a security key.

A passkey sign-in does not ask for your authenticator app's one-time code: the passkey already confirms that it is you, on your device. Your password and Google sign-in keep working. To remove a passkey, select **Delete** next to it under **Signing in**.
