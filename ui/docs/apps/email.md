# Email

Some apps send email, such as invitations, password resets and notifications.

- Apps send email from a Freepod address: `<app>@freepod.eu`, for example `immich@freepod.eu`. The sender address cannot be changed.
- Apps cannot receive email. Replies to their messages are not delivered to the app or to you.
- Messages can end up in the recipient's spam folder. Ask recipients to check it, or to add the sender address to their contacts.

The [product guides](/apps/products) list which apps send email.
