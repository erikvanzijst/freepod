# Getting help

## Feedback

To tell us what is wrong, what you miss, or what you like, use the **Feedback** tab on the right edge of the Freepod dashboard and website, or the **Feedback** link at the top of these docs. Choose a topic and write your message. A screenshot of the page is attached; you can remove it before sending. Feedback is sent from your Freepod account, together with the page's address, your browser and recent errors on the page. If you are not signed in, you can email [feedback@freepod.eu](mailto:feedback@freepod.eu) instead.

## Support

Email [support@freepod.eu](mailto:support@freepod.eu).

Include:

- the hostname of the app or deployment;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- any error message, exactly as shown;
- for developer deployments, the output of the failing `freepod` command and, if relevant, `freepod releases`.

Do not send passwords, master passwords, registration tokens or other secrets.

For questions about using an app itself, such as Nextcloud or Immich, its own documentation is linked from the [product guides](/apps/products).
